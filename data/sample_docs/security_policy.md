# Aurora Systems GmbH — Information Security Policy

All laptops must have full-disk encryption and endpoint protection enabled
before connecting to internal systems. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is
mandatory for all cloud services, including email, source control, and the
internal knowledge base.

Access to production systems requires a signed access request approved by the
relevant system owner and is reviewed quarterly. Access is automatically
revoked 24 hours after an employee's last working day.

Any suspected security incident — phishing email, lost device, unauthorized
access — must be reported to security@aurora-systems.example within one hour
of discovery. Incidents are triaged within four business hours.

Customer data classified as "Confidential" or higher may not be stored on
personal devices or transferred via unencrypted channels, including personal
email or consumer file-sharing tools.
