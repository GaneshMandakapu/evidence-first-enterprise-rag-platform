# Aurora Systems GmbH — Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager
approval recorded in the HR portal. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00
CET, during which employees are expected to be reachable on Slack and video
calls.

Employees working from outside Germany for more than 15 consecutive days must
notify HR at least one week in advance for tax and social-security compliance
reasons. Remote work from countries outside the EU requires additional legal
review and is approved on a case-by-case basis.

Home-office equipment (monitor, chair, docking station) can be requested once
per 24 months through the IT service desk, up to a budget of EUR 400.
