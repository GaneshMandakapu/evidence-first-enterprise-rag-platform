# Aurora Systems GmbH — New Hire Onboarding Guide

New employees receive their laptop and access credentials on day one via the
IT service desk. Accounts for email, Slack, source control, and the internal
knowledge base are provisioned automatically once the signed contract is
received by HR, typically 3–5 business days before the start date.

The first week follows a fixed onboarding track: company overview and values
(day 1), team introductions and tooling setup (day 2), security and
compliance training — mandatory, tracked in the LMS — (day 3), and a
first small project task assigned by the hiring manager (days 4–5).

Every new hire is assigned a buddy from their team for the first 90 days.
Probation review happens at the 3-month mark and is a two-way conversation,
not a one-sided evaluation.
